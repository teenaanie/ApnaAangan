# Applications outside the app

## Anything forwarded — rate cards, posters, one-pagers

Inline the token values as literal hex. A standalone HTML file gets emailed,
opened from a Downloads folder and printed; a stylesheet reference will not
survive that. Keep to the same palette and the same two faces, with web-safe
fallbacks named after the licensed fonts.

Print: cream background is expensive in ink and unreliable across printers.
For anything meant to be printed on paper, use white and let the terracotta and
mustard carry the brand.

## WhatsApp

Plain text, no markdown — asterisks show as asterisks in some clients. Short
lines, blank lines between ideas, because it is read on a phone at a gate.

Lead with what the person gets, not with pricing. The line that works is "you
get your own page and a QR code — send people one link instead of retyping your
menu every morning". Pricing is the second conversation.

## Email

Inline styles only. Cream `#f8f1e3` panel, `#333433` text, one terracotta
button with white text and a fully-round radius. Assume images are blocked, so
never put anything necessary in one.

## QR handouts

The provider's own share link, the mark, and their name. Nothing else. These
are handed over in a doorway.

## Awaiting from the guideline

Poster layouts, social templates, signage, any co-branding with a society.
